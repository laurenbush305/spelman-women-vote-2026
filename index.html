GEORGIA ONLY · NOVEMBER 2026 GENERAL ELECTION

Georgia, at a glance

These dates apply only to Georgia. Other states have their own rules and dates.

Oct 5Voter registration deadlineCheck official calendar
Oct 13–30Early in-person voting
Saturday voting: Oct 17 & 24Confirm local hours
Oct 23Absentee application must be received by your county election officeCheck application instructions
Nov 3 · 7 p.m.Standard domestic absentee ballot receipt deadline; Election Day polls: 7 a.m.–7 p.m.Check ballot return instructions

Registered Georgia voters do not need an excuse to request an absentee ballot. Follow the official application, signature and ID instructions. Mailing on the deadline is not the same as receipt. Military and overseas voters: follow FVAP guidance ↗ for applicable exceptions. Check your county for early voting locations and hours.

Important: The individual form is publicly linked on NAASC's 2026 campaign page. The chapter reporting URL remains unconfirmed; request it from gotv@naasc.org. The event form is for event submissions, not verified campaign outcome totals.
Before reporting: gather your chapter’s records
  • Chapter name, reporting period and an owner who can verify the report.
  • Activities and event dates; volunteers and volunteer hours with supporting logs.
  • People reached: distinguish unique people from repeated contacts or estimated impressions.
  • Photos and stories with permission to share. Avoid voter records, ballot choices or other sensitive voter details.
  • Confirm the national reporting deadline with NAASC; the source toolkit leaves it as [DEADLINE].

Website visits and resource clicks are engagement measures. They are not votes, confirmed registrations or submitted reports. National impact totals will require validated reports; none are displayed here.
